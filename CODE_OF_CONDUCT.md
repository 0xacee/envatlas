# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of positive behavior include empathy, constructive feedback, respect
for differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility. Harassment, trolling,
personal attacks, and publishing others' private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through
GitHub. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with respect for the
safety and privacy of the reporter.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
